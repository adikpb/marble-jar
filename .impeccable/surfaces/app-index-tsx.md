# Surface brief — Marble Jar shelf + jar (replacement world)

## Direction contract

THESIS: The shelf is a kitchen in the evening, not a dashboard. Each person's trust lives as a real jar of marble-dots on a wooden shelf, and logging a moment is as fast as sticking a note on the fridge — while the same screen invites sitting back with the week that just happened. This surface refuses the habit-tracker rut (streaks, scores, rings, leaderboard counts) and its predictable opposite, the pastel therapy-scrapbook (soft affirmations, washy gradients); neither gamifies nor sentimentalizes, and removals sit in the open as honest absences rather than failures.

OWN-WORLD: Lamplight enamel: a deep espresso ground that holds everything, warm paper slips carrying each moment's words, cherry-magnet red reserved strictly for live actions, and the seven BRAVING hues appearing only as marble glass and tag edges — never as chrome. Display names set big in Bricolage Grotesque, working text in Karla, moment reasons handwritten in Caveat on their slips. Marble-dot fill rows, round magnet pins, hairline rules, soft offset shadows with real blur like lamplight falloff. No cards-with-icons, no eyebrow kickers, no progress rings; headings carry their own weight.

STORY: The visitor understands within seconds that this is where small trust moments live, one jar per person, each marble backed by a reason and a BRAVING tag. They believe small moments count because they can see them — glossy dots with words attached, gaps where marbles were honestly removed. They do two things: log a kept or broken promise in seconds without leaving the shelf, and open a jar to sit with the week, the tag split, and the history that answers "how are we doing?"

FIRST VIEWPORT: The shelf opens on lamplight: the question "Whose jar are you filling?" set large, a quick-log composer directly beneath it (jar picker, reason line, tag row, add / remove as two honest equals), then a "this week" ribbon of the freshest marbles across every jar — reflection visible without scrolling. Below, each jar is its own shelf board: the name big, a row of twenty marble dots filling in BRAVING hues, the latest moment as a pinned slip. The primary action (log a moment) sits above the fold, not behind navigation.

FORM: Grounded candidate 3 of 7, seed key 9d8352a7 — the refrigerator-door domestic collage (layered notes, photos, magnets; glass, paper/ink, wood, lamplight families). Specimen raise: jar names and counts set at poster scale against small working labels. Catalog-grid raise: the history feed keeps a committed collector's density, every moment a tangible slip, never airy placeholders. Metro raise: tag selection as full-bleed hue fields, no timid chips. Factory-sleeve raise: chapters and moments carry visible sequence numbers, an honest catalog order. Laban raise: history grouped on a week staff with shaped add/remove marks so time reads as space. Ruling-engine raise: hairline rules and margin glosses throughout, states as printed marks.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
